# What is RunSAFi?

RunSAFi helps organizations build and run AI agents they can trust with real work. SAFi — an open-source governance engine for agentic AI — governs the agent's reasoning and actions at runtime, enforcing defined boundaries and leaving an immutable audit record behind.

- **Your AI. Your infrastructure. Your governance.** RunSAFi deploys the agents directly in the customer's environment, so the customer retains control of its AI, infrastructure, and data.
- **We operate, you retain control.** RunSAFi handles deployment, configuration, maintenance, monitoring, backups, and updates. The customer keeps ownership of the machine, the data, the model keys, and the governance records.
- **Operated by the framework's creator.** RunSAFi is operated by Nelson Amaya, the author of the Self-Alignment Framework and the architect of SAFi, with more than two decades of enterprise IT infrastructure experience.

## What the customer keeps

- Your Infrastructure
- Your Data
- Your AI Model API Keys
- Your Governance Records

## How the model works

RunSAFi deploys the governed agents into the customer's secure environment. The RunSAFi Engine operates and monitors the agents; the agents themselves run inside the customer's environment.

## How the pieces relate

- **SAF** is the framework — the Self-Alignment Framework, a philosophical system for governing autonomous systems.
- **SAFi** is the engine — the open-source technical implementation: a runtime governance engine for AI agents.
- **RunSAFi** is the service — the business that brings SAFi into real organizations, building, deploying, and operating AI agents around real business use cases.
