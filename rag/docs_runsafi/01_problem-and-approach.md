# The Problem RunSAFi Solves

AI agents are becoming capable of doing real work: researching, analyzing information, interacting with systems, making decisions, and executing business processes. But giving an AI agent access to real systems creates a different problem: how do you let an AI do useful work without giving up control?

That is the problem RunSAFi exists to solve. RunSAFi helps organizations build and run AI agents they can trust with real work. We bring the tech. You bring the resources and use cases.

## Mission and core values

> Our mission is to empower organizations to harness the full potential of AI without ever compromising control over their data, infrastructure, or code.

Core values:
- **Transparency.** Everything we build is either available under an open-source license or deployed directly in the client's environment without restrictions. Technology is built for review, inspection, and understanding.
- **Human-Centric Solutions.** Everything we build starts with a human need. AI augments the team, not replaces their judgment.
- **Data Sovereignty.** Organizations remain in control of their AI — what it can access, what it can do, where it runs, what information it uses, and what happens to their data.

## An infrastructure mindset

RunSAFi was built from more than two decades of enterprise IT experience. Traditional infrastructure operates on principles that are easy to understand:

- Least privilege
- Defined boundaries
- Separation of responsibilities
- Auditability
- Controlled access
- Human accountability

AI agents introduce a different kind of system. Their capabilities are powerful, but their behavior is not something you should govern with prompts alone. We believe AI agents need an operational governance layer that sits between what an agent wants to do and what it is actually allowed to do. That is where SAFi comes in.

## SAFi: Governance at runtime

SAFi is the open-source governance engine behind RunSAFi. It provides the governance layer that evaluates an agent's reasoning and actions against defined policies before allowing those actions to proceed. The goal isn't to make AI predictable — the goal is to make the boundaries around AI predictable.

- Enforce defined policies
- Control tool access
- Require human approval
- Maintain an auditable record of what the agent did and why

That allows organizations to use capable AI systems while maintaining control over how those systems operate.

## AI should operate where your business operates

Your data is yours. Your infrastructure is yours. Your business processes are yours. RunSAFi designs deployments to operate within the customer's environment whenever the use case requires it. The customer controls the infrastructure, the data, and the model credentials. RunSAFi brings the technology and expertise to build, deploy, secure, monitor, and maintain the system. When the team is ready, the system can be handed over to the organization.

## From framework to working systems

- **SAF** — the philosophy. The Self-Alignment Framework explores how values, reasoning, intention, judgment, and action can be structured into a framework for governing autonomous systems.
- **SAFi** — the technology. SAFi turns those ideas into an open-source governance engine for AI agents.
- **RunSAFi** — the service. RunSAFi brings SAFi into real organizations and builds, deploys, and operates AI agents around real business use cases.

SAF is the framework. SAFi is the engine. RunSAFi is the business.

## The founder

RunSAFi was founded by Nelson Amaya, the original author of the Self-Alignment Framework and the architect of its open-source implementation SAFi. After a 22-year career in enterprise IT spanning roles from the help desk to IT Director — managing complex cloud environments and leading service desk operations — he built RunSAFi to solve a specific problem: how to deploy highly capable AI agents that align with an organization's mission and values while strictly adhering to enterprise security and regulatory compliance standards.

## Not there to replace your IT team

RunSAFi helps the organization put AI to work. The customer knows its business, systems, and where the valuable work is. RunSAFi brings the technology and engineering expertise to turn those opportunities into working AI agents within the boundaries the organization requires.

## Built for real work

The future of AI is not simply better chatbots. The interesting question is what happens when AI starts doing actual work — researching, analyzing, processing, updating systems, coordinating workflows, making recommendations, and taking actions. That is where AI becomes genuinely useful, and where governance becomes essential.
