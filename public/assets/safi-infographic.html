What happens on a single turn

Values — the standard, not a step

An organisation's Charter and a unit's Policy are compiled into one scored value set before the turn begins, and held read-only while it runs. Nothing below can alter what it is judged against while being judged.

Will screens the incoming prompt, before any model is called
Intellect drafts an answer, or proposes a tool call — and can execute nothing
Will authorises a proposed tool, checking the arguments and not just the name
Will checks the draft's structure — required disclaimers, permitted syntax
Conscience scores the draft value by value against the rubrics, with a reason and a confidence for each
Will enforces the hard gates — a violation here blocks outright
Spirit folds the scores into the agent's long-term alignment and measures drift
Will rules on the alignment figure against a fixed threshold

Every stage runs before the answer reaches you — the audit is a control, not a report. The Will appears five times, marked by the gold edge: governing an agent that can act means authorising before the action, not only reviewing after the result.

Why the record can be trusted

One idea does most of the work, and it is a negative one. Each faculty is refused the information that would let it do another's job.

FacultyDoesIs denied
ValuesSupplies the standard Nothing — it is the standard, compiled before the turn and read-only while it runs
IntellectDrafts The rubrics it will be judged against, and any score
WillAuthorises and blocks Meaning itself — it holds no language model and cannot read semantics
ConscienceJudges The weights, so it cannot reason about downstream consequences
SpiritRemembers and measures Authority — it computes, and the Will decides

And when something is missing, it refuses

A hard-gate value the audit never scored is a violation. An audit that failed is a violation. A constrained tool parameter left unspecified is a refusal. None of these are worked around, because the alternative is shipping something ungoverned and calling it governed.

No faculty in the chain is ever in a position to write its own verdict. That is what makes the record at the end of a turn worth reading.
